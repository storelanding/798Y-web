Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how INCAI ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Ai Translator mobile application for Android, distributed on Google Play (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Accessibility Service

The App uses the Android AccessibilityService API to help you capture text displayed on your screen from other applications. This enables translation of that content into your preferred language in real time (the Screen Translator feature).

  • The Accessibility Service is used only to provide the translation feature, and only after you enable it in your device settings.
  • The App does not collect, store or share any personal or sensitive data through this feature. Processed information is used solely to provide the translation and is not retained or transmitted externally.
  • You can turn the Accessibility Service off at any time in your device's Settings → Accessibility.

2. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. These services may collect information, such as device identifiers and usage data, according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertising in the App. Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network — displays advertising in the App. Meta Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — helps us understand how the App is used so we can improve it. Firebase privacy information
  • Google Analytics — usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Adjust — measures app installs and advertising campaign performance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports so we can fix stability issues. Firebase privacy information

3. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. These networks may use your device's Advertising ID to show personalized ads and to measure ad performance.

  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to your interests.

4. Subscriptions

The App offers an optional in-app subscription. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment card details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Data storage and security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, information related to your use of the App by emailing us at ltinc9986@gmail.com. For information collected by the third-party services listed above, you can also use the controls those services provide.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when the updated policy is posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
