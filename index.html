Tap to Speak

Speak naturally, get instant voice translation

Tap the mic and talk — ordering food in Italy or chatting with a driver in Japan. Translation starts when you pause, and you can hear the result read aloud. Your phone becomes a pocket interpreter.

  • Tap to speak, or hold to talk in noisy places
  • Tap a word to fix what you said before translating
  • Listen at adjustable speed, copy or save to favorites
  • "Explain with AI" for meaning, tone and other ways to say it
Live Conversation

Face-to-face talk with anyone, anywhere

Put the phone between you and a local. The split screen shows each person their own language, so both sides can read and listen to the conversation as it happens.

  • Mirrored layout — the other person reads their half the right way up
  • Manual or Auto mode for taking turns
  • Read aloud on, plus quick phrases one tap away
Image Translate

Point your camera at signs and documents

Aim at a station sign, a notice or a form. The app finds the text, translates it in place and lists every line so you can listen, copy or edit it.

  • Live, Capture and Document modes
  • Switch between Translated and Original views
  • Import photos from your gallery
  • "Ask AI" about what a sign or notice means
Travel scenarios

Order food, read signs, handle the hotel

Jump straight into the situation you are in. Scenario cards cover menus (dishes & allergens), signs, documents, shopping, hotels and emergencies — and in Order food mode your allergies stay visible while you scan the menu.

  • Menu, Signs, Documents, Shopping, Hotel and Emergency cards
  • Personal allergy reminder when scanning menus
  • Emergency card is always on
Phrase Dialogue

Travel phrases ready when you need them

Curated phrases for travel, shopping, hotels and emergencies help you feel confident in every interaction — even if you are just starting to learn the language. Tap to hear each phrase spoken.

  • Quick phrases with audio playback
  • Phrase of the day with pronunciation
  • Recent translations kept on the home screen
  • Search 100+ languages by name or native script

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Ai Translator.

What can I translate with Ai Translator?

Speech, typed text, photos from your camera or gallery, and text shown on your screen in other apps. You can also use the face-to-face conversation mode, curated travel phrases and the smart dictionary.

How many languages are supported?

More than 100 languages, from English and Spanish to Vietnamese and Arabic. Pick any pair on the language screen and swap them with one tap.

How does the conversation mode work?

Open Live Conversation and place the phone between you and the other person. Each side of the screen shows one language, mirrored so the other person can read their half. Choose Manual or Auto mode for taking turns.

Why does the app ask for the Accessibility Service?

The Screen Translator uses Android's AccessibilityService API to capture text displayed on your screen from other apps so it can be translated into your preferred language in real time. The app does not collect, store or share personal or sensitive data through this feature. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Is there a paid version?

Yes, an optional Premium subscription is available inside the app. It is billed through Google Play and can be cancelled anytime in your Google Play subscriptions.

Which devices are supported?

Ai Translator is an Android app distributed on Google Play.
